American defence technology firm Red 6 has announced a new partnership with Northrop Grumman Corporation on the Beacon initiative, a next-generation open-access testbed designed to accelerate the development of autonomous mission capabilities. The collaboration will integrate Red 6’s Advanced Tactical Augmented Reality System (ATARS) into the Beacon platform.
